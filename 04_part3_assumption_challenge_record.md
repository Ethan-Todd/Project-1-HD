## Causal case logic

- Downside links weak housing affordability and big-project demand to lower organic growth, fixed-cost deleverage, gross-margin pressure, and continued capex commitments.
- Base uses FY2026 guidance midpoints and lets acquisition/new-unit growth fade while repair/remodel demand normalizes.
- Upside links stronger project demand and acquisition integration to higher organic growth, better gross margin and SG&A leverage, with higher reinvestment rather than free growth.

These are conditional paths, not probabilities. Joint movement preserves economic causality but does not prove each magnitude is equally likely.
